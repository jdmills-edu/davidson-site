Davidson, North Carolina

A charming college town on the shores of Lake Norman

Things to Do

Davidson College

Stroll the beautiful campus of this top-ranked liberal arts college. Catch a basketball game at John M. Belk Arena, see a show at Duke Family Performance Hall, or explore the college's galleries and gardens.

Lake Norman

The largest man-made lake in the Carolinas offers boating, fishing, kayaking, and paddleboarding. Rent watercraft for the day or enjoy the views from shore.

Davidson Farmers Market

Every Saturday from 9 a.m. to noon, local farmers, bakers, and artisans line the street next to Town Hall with fresh produce, baked goods, and handmade wares.

Davidson Greenway

A 6-mile loop for walking, running, and biking with entry points throughout Davidson and neighboring Cornelius. A great way to explore the area on foot.

Downtown Dining

Locally owned restaurants line the walkable downtown. Highlights include Flatiron (new American), The Pickled Peach (sandwiches and soups), Il Bosco (Italian), and Summit Coffee.

Loch Norman Highland Games

Held annually at Rural Hill farm, this event features traditional Scottish athletic competitions, Highland dancing, Celtic music, and food.

Performing Arts

The Davidson Community Players stage productions at the Armour Street Theater, while Davidson College hosts concerts, lectures, and exhibitions throughout the academic year at Duke Family Performance Hall.
