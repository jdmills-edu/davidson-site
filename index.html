Welcome to Davidson

Davidson is a small town in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina, situated about 20 miles north of Charlotte along the eastern shore of Lake Norman, the largest man-made lake in the Carolinas. With a population of roughly 15,000, Davidson blends small-town Southern charm with the cultural vitality of a college community.

Home to Davidson College, a nationally ranked liberal arts institution founded in 1837, the town's identity is deeply intertwined with the college. Tree-lined streets, locally owned shops, and a walkable downtown give Davidson a character that draws visitors and new residents alike.
